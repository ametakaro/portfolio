From Local Struggle to Global Cause: Framing Strategies in the Baloch Long March Movement

Meneliti bagaimana gerakan Baloch Long March (2023–2024) membingkai perjuangan HAM lokal agar mendapat perhatian dunia. Saya memakai analisis isi kualitatif dengan model framing Snow & Benford atas unggahan media sosial, materi kampanye, pernyataan publik, dan liputan pers.

Examines how the Baloch Long March (2023–2024) framed a local human rights struggle to win global attention. I used qualitative content analysis with Snow & Benford's framing model on social media posts, campaign materials, public statements, and press coverage.

Temuannya: dengan membingkai ulang tuntutan sebagai isu HAM universal, lewat aksi yang dipimpin perempuan, poster, dan tagar, gerakan lokal bisa menggerakkan jaringan diaspora dan advokasi global.

Finding: by reframing grievances as universal human rights issues, through women-led rallies, posters, and hashtags, a local movement mobilized diaspora networks and global advocacy.

The Boomerang Mechanism in Human Rights Advocacy: Baloch Long March Against China-Pakistan Economic Corridor

Saat jalur dalam negeri tertutup, aktivis Baloch Yakjehti Committee membawa tuntutan atas penghilangan paksa ke panggung internasional lewat organisasi HAM dan media global. Tekanan ini berujung pada pernyataan para pakar PBB di awal 2025. Artikel ini membacanya dengan model "boomerang" advokasi transnasional.

With domestic channels blocked, Baloch Yakjehti Committee activists took their demands over enforced disappearances to the world stage through rights groups and global media, prompting statements from UN experts in early 2025. The article reads this through the "boomerang" model of transnational advocacy.
